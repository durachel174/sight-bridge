# SightBridge User Interview Script

Duration: about 35 minutes.

## Part 1: Current Experience

- Tell me about the last time you needed visual help with something. Walk me through it.
- Have you ever decided not to ask for visual help because of what was visible?
- When you use Be My Eyes or a similar service, do you think about what the volunteer can see beyond what you are asking about?
- Have you ever prepared your space before a visual assistance call by moving or covering something?

## Part 2: Sensitivity Perceptions

Read each prompt and ask how the participant would feel about a stranger seeing it:

- Mail with their address
- Prescription bottle
- Bank statement
- Laptop screen
- Family photos
- Grocery list
- ID card

Ask:

- Are there situations where you would want help only from AI, not a human?
- If an AI told you "a credit card is visible in frame" before connecting to a volunteer, would that change your behavior?

## Part 3: Product Reaction

- Imagine a system that tells you what sensitive information might be visible before sharing your camera. How would that feel?
- Would you want to be told every time, only sometimes, or only for certain categories?
- If the system said "to read this prescription, I will send the image to a cloud AI service; no humans will see it," does that feel different from just saying "reading privately"?
- Which categories would you turn on: financial, medical, personal ID, addresses, screens, documents?

## Part 4: Close

- Is there anything about privacy and visual assistance that I have not asked about but should have?
- If this product existed today, would you use it?
- What would make you stop using it?
